About Rwanda Coding Academy

Cultivating the next generation of tech leaders and innovators in Rwanda

Building a Digital Future

Rwanda Coding Academy (RCA) is a specialized center of excellence that trains young Rwandan talents in software programming, embedded systems, and network security. Founded in 2019, the academy is an initiative of the Government of Rwanda to create a pool of technical talent to drive the country's digital transformation.

Located in Nyabihu District, Western Province, the academy offers a three-year program for selected high-performing students who have demonstrated exceptional aptitude in mathematics, physics, and computer science. The curriculum is designed to equip students with practical skills and theoretical knowledge needed to excel in the tech industry.

Production of Artificial Rainfall

Students developing cloud seeding technology to induce rainfall in drought-prone areas of Rwanda.

Field testing the artificial rainfall equipment in partnership with the Rwanda Meteorology Agency.

Successful demonstration of the artificial rainfall system with government officials in attendance.

The Artificial Rainfall project is a groundbreaking initiative led by senior students at Rwanda Coding Academy. Using advanced meteorological algorithms and drone technology, students have developed a sustainable solution to address agricultural challenges during dry seasons. This innovation has already attracted interest from neighboring countries facing similar climate challenges.

Construction of the University of Rwanda Coding Academy

Initial groundbreaking ceremony with Rwanda's Minister of Education and technology industry partners.

Construction of the main academic building featuring state-of-the-art laboratories and learning spaces.

Digital rendering of the completed campus expected to host 500 students by 2027.

The University of Rwanda Coding Academy represents the next evolution in technology education for Rwanda. This purpose-built campus will expand on the success of Rwanda Coding Academy by offering advanced degrees in Software Engineering, AI & Machine Learning, and Digital Innovation. Construction began in January 2025 with funding from both government and private sector partners, reflecting Rwanda's commitment to becoming a technology hub for East Africa.
